Getting Started
Do I need a license to get started?+
You must be licensed to sell, but you can start the interview process, onboarding, and training before you pass your exam. Most states allow you to get licensed in 1–3 weeks. We'll guide you through every step.
Is there a cost to join Omnia?+
There is no buy-in, no franchise fee, and no charge to join our organization. Standard costs include your state licensing exam fee (~$40–$100) and E&O insurance (~$200–$400/year). That's it — and those are standard industry costs for any independent agent.
Is this 1099 or W2?+
This is a 1099 independent contractor position. You are your own business. That means full scheduling flexibility, the ability to build your own team, and access to significant tax advantages that W2 employees don't get.
Can I work part-time while keeping my current job?+
Absolutely. Many of our top agents started part-time — 10–15 hours a week — while keeping their day job. Once your insurance income exceeds your current salary, most choose to transition full-time. We support you at every stage.
Leads & Sales
Do you provide leads, or do I have to find my own?+
We have a robust warm lead program. Leads are available for purchase at cost — these are pre-qualified, exclusive leads from people who have recently requested information about life insurance. You are not cold calling strangers. Ever.
How fast can I make my first sale?+
Most agents who follow the system make their first sale within 7–14 days of becoming active. Some do it on day one. Results depend on effort, consistency, and following the proven process your mentor will teach you.
What carriers do you work with?+
We are contracted with 20+ top-rated A+ carriers including some of the most recognized names in the life insurance industry. Having multiple carrier options means you can always find the right product for each client's situation — which translates directly to more sales.
Is everything really 100% remote?+
Yes. Every aspect of the job — presentations, applications, training, team meetings — is done virtually. You work from your home, a coffee shop, or wherever you have a laptop and internet connection. We operate in all 50 states.
Compensation & Growth
How does the commission structure work?+
Commissions are paid as a percentage of the first-year premium for each policy you sell. Omnia offers among the highest commission levels in the industry. Your exact rate depends on your contract level, which scales up as you produce. Full transparency — no hidden deductions.
Can I build my own team and earn overrides?+
Yes. Once you're established as a producing agent, you can recruit and train your own agents and earn override commissions on everything they produce. This is how top earners build income that compounds over time. There is no limit to how large your team can grow.
What does the average agent actually earn?+
Part-time agents (5–15 hrs/week) typically earn $2,000–$5,000/month. Full-time agents earn $6,000–$15,000/month. Senior agents with small teams earn $15,000–$30,000+/month. These are realistic ranges based on active agent data — not cherry-picked outliers.
When and how do I get paid?+
Commissions are paid directly by the carrier — typically within 5–10 business days of a policy being issued. There is no middleman holding your money. Most agents receive their first commission check within 2–3 weeks of their first sale.
Training & Support
What training do you provide?+
New agents go through our Omnia Fast-Start program — a structured onboarding covering scripts, product knowledge, running virtual presentations, objection handling, and how to use our systems. After that, ongoing weekly training calls, a private agent community, and 1-on-1 mentorship are all available.
Do I need sales experience?+
No. Many of our most successful agents had zero sales background before joining Omnia. What matters most is coachability, work ethic, and willingness to follow the system. We'll teach you everything else.
